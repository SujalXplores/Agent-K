# How proration works when adding seats mid-cycle

Adding a new member mid-cycle bills you a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period, calculated as (days remaining in cycle / total days in cycle) x monthly seat price. This prorated charge appears as a separate line item on your next invoice, not as an immediate charge.

Removing a member mid-cycle does not issue a prorated refund - the seat remains billed until the end of the current cycle, after which it drops off the next invoice automatically. This asymmetry (charge on add, no refund on remove) is intentional and documented in the pricing terms.

If you add and remove the same seat count within one cycle, you are billed once for the addition and nothing further - there's no double counting across the two invoices.
