# What happens when my free trial expires

Flowdeck's free trial runs 14 days from workspace creation, no credit card required to start. On day 15, if no plan has been selected, the workspace enters a 3-day read-only grace period: members can view existing boards and export data but cannot create or edit tasks.

After the read-only grace period, the workspace is fully locked (no viewing) until a paid plan is selected, but no data is deleted - everything reactivates immediately once billing is set up. Flowdeck sends trial-ending reminder emails on day 10, day 13, and the morning the read-only period begins.
