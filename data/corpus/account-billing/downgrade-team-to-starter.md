# Downgrading from Team to Starter plan

You can downgrade at any time from Workspace Settings > Billing > Plan > "Change plan". The Starter plan caps active projects at 5 and members at 10, so Flowdeck will first show you which projects or members would need to be archived or removed to fit under those limits before letting the downgrade proceed.

The downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle, not immediately - you keep Team-plan features until then. No credit is issued for the unused portion of the current cycle; Flowdeck does not prorate downgrades, only plan changes that increase seats mid-cycle (see the prorated-billing-mid-cycle doc).
