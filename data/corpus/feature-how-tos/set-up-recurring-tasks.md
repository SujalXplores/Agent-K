# Setting up recurring tasks

Open any task's detail panel and toggle "Make recurring" to choose a frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, or a custom cron-like schedule). When a recurring task is marked Done, Flowdeck automatically creates the next occurrence at the configured interval rather than leaving the completed instance around and expecting you to duplicate it manually.

Recurring tasks preserve their custom fields, assignee, and checklist template on each new occurrence, but comments and attachments do not carry forward - each occurrence starts with a clean comment thread, which keeps recurring operational tasks (like "weekly deploy checklist") from accumulating years of stale comments.
