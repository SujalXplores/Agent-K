# Inviting external guests with limited access

Guest accounts (available on Team and Enterprise plans) let you invite external collaborators - clients, contractors, vendors - to specific projects without giving them workspace-wide visibility into other projects or the member directory. Invite a guest from a specific project's Members tab using "Invite guest" rather than the workspace-level "Invite member" flow.

Guests can be scoped as Viewer or Editor on the specific project(s) they're invited to, but can never be granted project-Admin or any workspace-level role, and they cannot see the list of other members or guests on a project unless they're mentioned in a task or comment.
