# What are your pricing tiers?

We offer three pricing tiers: 1) Starter ($9/month) — 1 user, 5GB storage, email support. 2) Pro ($29/month) — 5 users, 50GB storage, priority email support, advanced analytics. 3) Enterprise ($99/month) — unlimited users, 500GB storage, phone support, custom integrations, SSO. All plans include a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. Annual billing gets a 20% discount. Custom pricing is available for organizations over 100 users.
