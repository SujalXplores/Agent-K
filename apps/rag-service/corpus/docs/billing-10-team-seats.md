# How do I add or remove team seats?

To add team seats, go to Settings > Team > Manage Seats. Click "Add Seats" and select how many additional seats you need. You'll be charged a pro-rated amount for the remainder of the billing period. To remove seats, first remove the team members occupying them, then reduce the seat count. Removed seats take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. You cannot remove seats that are currently assigned to active team members.
