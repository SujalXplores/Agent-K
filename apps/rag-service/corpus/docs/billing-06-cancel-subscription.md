# How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your subscription, go to Settings > Billing > Subscription and click "Cancel Subscription." Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period — you won't be charged again. You can re-subscribe at any time. If you cancel within the 30-day money-back window, you'll receive a full refund. Cancellation is immediate for monthly plans and effective at the end of the billing cycle for annual plans.
