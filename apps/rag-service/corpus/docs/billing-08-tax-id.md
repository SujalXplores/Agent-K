# How do I add a tax ID to my invoices?

To add a tax ID (VAT, GST, EIN, etc.) to your invoices, go to Settings > Billing > Tax Information. Enter your tax ID number and select the type. The tax ID will appear on all future invoices. For past invoices, contact support and we can re-issue them with your tax ID included. This is especially important for EU customers who need VAT-compliant invoices.
