# I'm locked out of my account. What should I do?

If you've entered the wrong password too many times, your account may be temporarily locked for security. Wait 15 minutes and try again. If you're still locked out after waiting, use the "Forgot Password" link to reset your password. If you believe your account was locked in error, contact support with your username and the email associated with your account. We can unlock it manually after verifying your identity.
