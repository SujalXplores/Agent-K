# How does team collaboration work?

Team collaboration features are available on Pro and Enterprise plans. You can: 1) Share items with team members via shared collections. 2) Leave comments and annotations on shared items. 3) Assign tasks and track progress. 4) View activity feeds to see what your team is working on. Team members must be invited via email and accept the invitation to join. Each team member has their own login but shares the team's subscription and resources.
