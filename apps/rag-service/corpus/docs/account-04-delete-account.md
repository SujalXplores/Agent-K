# How do I delete my account?

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. To delete your account, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. You'll be asked to enter your password to confirm. All your data, including order history and saved preferences, will be permanently removed within 30 days. If you have an active subscription, it will be cancelled immediately and you won't be charged again. Consider exporting your data before deletion if you need it later.
