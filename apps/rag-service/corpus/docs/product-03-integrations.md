# What integrations are available?

We offer native integrations with: Slack (notifications), Salesforce (CRM sync), HubSpot (contact sync), Zapier (5000+ apps), Microsoft Teams (notifications), and Google Calendar (scheduling). Enterprise plans also support custom integrations via our REST API and webhooks. To set up an integration, go to Settings > Integrations and click "Connect" next to the service you want to integrate. Most integrations require OAuth authorization.
