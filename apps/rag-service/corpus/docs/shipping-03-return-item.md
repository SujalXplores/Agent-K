# How do I return an item?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. To initiate a return, go to Orders > select the order > "Request Return." Choose your reason and print the prepaid return label. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier location. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days of receiving the returned item. Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
