# Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and weight. Delivery times vary by country: Canada/Mexico (7-10 business days), Europe (10-15 business days), Asia/Pacific (15-20 business days). International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient. Some items may be restricted in certain countries — check the product page for details.
