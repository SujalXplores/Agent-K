# What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact support within 30 days of your purchase for a full refund. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. Note that refunds are not available for: 1) accounts terminated for terms of service violations, 2) purchases older than 30 days, or 3) partial-month usage after the 30-day window. Annual plans are refunded on a pro-rated basis.
