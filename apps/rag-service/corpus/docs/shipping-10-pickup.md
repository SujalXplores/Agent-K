# Can I pick up my order in person?

Yes, we offer in-store pickup at select locations. During checkout, select "Pickup" as the shipping method and choose your preferred store location. You'll receive an email when your order is ready for pickup (usually within 2-4 hours during business hours). Bring your order confirmation and a photo ID. Orders must be picked up within 7 days or they'll be cancelled and refunded. Pickup is free with no minimum order.
