# Why does my session keep timing out?

For security, your session automatically expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. If you're being logged out more frequently, check the following: 1) Are you using a VPN that changes your IP address? 2) Is your browser set to block cookies? 3) Are you logged in on multiple devices? If none of these apply, try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then log in again. If the issue persists, contact support.
