# How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your registered email address and we will send you a reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder. If you still can't find it, contact support and we can resend the reset email manually. After clicking the link, you'll be prompted to enter a new password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
