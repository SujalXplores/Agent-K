# Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

You can change your shipping address if the order hasn't been shipped yet. Go to Orders > select the order > "Edit Shipping Address." If the order has already been shipped, we can't change the address, but you can contact the carrier directly to request a redirect (fees may apply). If the package is returned to us due to an incorrect address, we'll contact you to arrange re-shipping at no additional cost.
