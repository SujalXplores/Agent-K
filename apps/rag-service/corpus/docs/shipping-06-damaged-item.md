# My item arrived damaged. What should I do?

We're sorry your item arrived damaged. Please contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the damaged item. We'll send a replacement at no cost and provide a prepaid return label for the damaged item. If you prefer a refund instead of a replacement, let us know. Do not dispose of the damaged item or its packaging until we've resolved the issue — we may need it for the carrier claim.
